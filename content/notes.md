Some of my working notes. I tried to write my notes to be pedagogical. If you feel confused, please refer to related recording videos if exist, or feel free to contact me.

### [Notes on Argyres-Douglas Theories](https://www.overleaf.com/read/kqvjhkxgyjbf#8f4dea)

Include the SUSY algebra, solitons and central charge extension, Seiberg-Witten theory, BPS geometry, Hitchin moduli space(to be completed), Class S and IIB construction of Argyres-Douglas theories, representation for the irregular states.

### [Notes on Instantons](https://www.overleaf.com/read/kfjqzbvgrkwp#1029d4)

Notes based on Nekrasov's talk on instanton partition functions in the Prestring summer school 2026, and related topics.

### [Notes on Spin Geometry and K-Theory](https://www.overleaf.com/read/xsmrxnkjfmkz#9d841a)

Notes on §1.1-§1.3 of *Spin Geometry* by Lawson and Michelsohn, §2.1 §2.2 of Hatcher's book *Vector Bundles & K-Theory*.

### [Notes on Homological Algebra](notes/homological-algebra/)

Notes on chapter 1 and 2 of Weibel's book *An Introduction to Homological Algebra*, include abelian categories, exact sequences, chain homotopy, resolutions, and derived functors. Handwritten notes and an edited LaTeX version are available.
