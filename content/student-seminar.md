This is the student-organized seminar series at Fudan University and SIMIS. The topics are mainly in mathematics and theoretical physics. You could find the recordings of the seminars in the following list and the [playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLF7gJfFJdnBY) on the YouTube channel.

Chapter 1 of Weibel's book.

### [Homological Algebra — Lecture 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hRs1JSN4oPE)

*Sep 27, 2026*

Introduce the delta-functor and the projective resolution.

### [Homological Algebra — Lecture 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BTtkhoMhtlg)

*Oct 11, 2026*

Complete the analysis of projective resolution, comparison theorem, horseshoe lemma and rush course about the derived functors.

### [Toric Variety — Chapter 1, Lecture 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b6-ADAes4Go)

*Nov 1, 2026*

Rush review of algebra geometry, introduction to the construction of toric variety from the fans, and many examples.

### [Toric Variety — Chapter 1, Lecture 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b_hQRU8XYgE)

*Nov 8, 2026*

Regarding the tedious proof of chapter 1.2 convex polyhedral cone, and basic supplement to chapter 1.3 affine toric variety, including the construction of the toric action.

### Topological K-Theory

*July 11, 2026*

Vector bundles, Grothendieck functor, and Bott periodicity; following Hatcher.

### [Infinitesimal Deformation of Complex Structure](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=539dNOsb4jw)

*May 11, 2026*

Discussed the Kodaira-Spencer map on the differentiable family, and the analysis of non-trivial case that dim H^1 might jumps. Ref: Kodaira, *Complex Manifold and Deformation of Complex Structures*.

### [Categorical Aspects of TQFT — Part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXMUUZw8Ju8)

*Oct 1, 2025*

Introduced Modular Tensor Category and Reshetikhin-Turaev invariants with backgrounds on 3d manifold.

### [Categorical Aspects of TQFT — Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hfOWyLwI5Ag)

*Oct 12, 2025*

Introduced category-extended TQFT and quantum double (Drinfeld Center) of monoidal category, as a process to obtain a braiding structure based on given monoidal category, and ribbon structure based on given dual structure.

### [Categorical Aspects of TQFT — Part 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PWK3jv8AFJs)

*Oct 26, 2025*

Introduced the Hopf algebra, then performed quantum deformation of $U (\mathfrak{sl}_2)$, especially on thecoproduct structure, to construct the quantum group $U_q (\mathfrak{sl}_2)$. Then discussed its representation and the Drinfeld Double of Borel subalgebra to obtain the whole quantum group. Ref: [arxiv:2106.05252](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05252).
